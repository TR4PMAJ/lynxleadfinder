Business-location snapshot: Overture Maps Places
Geographic scope: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania and nearby areas
Refresh cadence: monthly, subject to the upstream Overture release and successful snapshot build

SOURCE AND MODIFICATIONS
This snapshot is derived from the Overture Maps Foundation Places theme. It has been filtered to a Philadelphia-area bounding box, normalized into a compact record format, and deduplicated by name, address, and category. The source identifiers and source-license metadata available in the upstream records are retained in the snapshot.
